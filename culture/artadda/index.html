The idea

A gallery for one Sunday a month

Art Adda is a monthly art popup at Good Earth Malhar & Other Community Spaces. Once every second Sunday, resident artists bring their own work – paintings, photography, murals, illustration – and stand beside it to talk about how it was made.

There's no white cube. Work leans against the wall by the billing counter, hangs from rope and clothespins strung near the wash basin, or perches on an easel by the door. It's woven straight into the regular Sunday crowd – breakfast in the morning, snacks in the evening – so an ordinary gathering becomes, for a few hours, a gallery.

“
Art shouldn't wait behind gallery glass. At Adda it meets you mid-Sunday — between the coffee, the conversation and the corner where you wait for your order.
– Sneha M L, co-founder

Art Adda was founded in March 2025 by Sneha M L and Sharada Arun, who turned a first show of four friends into a Sunday ritual that's grown month by month since. Read more on the vision page ↗.

The Sunday rhythm

Venue
Good Earth Malhar & Other Community Spaces
When
Every 2nd Sunday
Morning session
7:00 – 10:30 AM
Evening session
4:00 – 7:30 PM

Set-up starts around 6:30 AM, about 30 minutes once an artist knows what goes where. Everyone stays through both sessions to talk with visitors, take feedback, and tell the story behind each piece.

Bring your work to a Sunday

Any resident artist can show at Art Adda. Submit your work and the guidelines will walk you through the practical side, how to display, price, and protect a piece in a food-and-coffee space.
