Good Earth Malhar · Culture

Connections

by Rohini Rajagopal

Twelve true stories of what makes a community, gathered from the residents of Good Earth Malhar. What does a boulder mean to a child? How can a night walk become a return to the self? Why might the exchange of hibiscus flowers signal a deep yet unspoken connection? What does borrowing banana leaves from a neighbour have to do with belonging?
